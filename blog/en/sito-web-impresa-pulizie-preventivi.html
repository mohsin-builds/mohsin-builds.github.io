Cleaning Company Website: How to Get Better Quote Requests

A cleaning-company website should explain your services and help suitable customers request a realistic quote.

INSIGHTS / ENGLISH GUIDE

A cleaning-company website should explain your services and help suitable customers request a realistic quote.

Explain the cleaning work you actually offer

Office cleaning, domestic visits, recurring work and post-renovation cleaning require different information. Explain what each service includes, where you work and whether a site visit is necessary. Avoid fixed prices before assessing size, conditions and frequency.

Make quote requests easy

Ask for the property type, area, required service, estimated frequency and a way to respond. Optional fields can cover special requirements. Confirm receipt, explain what happens next and test whether the message reaches the right business inbox from a mobile phone.

Build trust with real evidence

Use authentic photographs and verified credentials. Clearly state contact details, scope and limitations. Never invent testimonials, coverage or customer results; avoid nearly identical pages created only by changing city names.
