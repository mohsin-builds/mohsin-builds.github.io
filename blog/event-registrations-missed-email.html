Anxiety before an event. It does not automatically mean the whole website must be rebuilt: first identify the point where the customer journey breaks.

Diagnose before buying another tool

Collect essential registration details, capacity and a contact method; distinguish registration requests from confirmed places.

Record the relevant page, device, message and person responsible. A visual impression alone is not a diagnosis.

A low-cost, testable improvement

Test a full-capacity scenario, cancellation and waiting-list communication before opening registrations.

Assign an owner and run a real mobile test with someone unfamiliar with the business.

What not to promise

Avoid collecting unnecessary information or making payment claims unsupported by the workflow.

Where legal, privacy or financial rules apply, verify your obligations with a qualified professional rather than treating a blog post as specialist advice.

When professional help is worth considering

Write down a concrete outcome: fewer lost inquiries, more accurate information, or a booking process that reflects real availability. Collect basic evidence before approving a rebuild.

Ask exactly what pages will change, how the improvement will be tested and who will operate it afterward. Preserve useful URLs and working features; do not pay for an unowned process.
